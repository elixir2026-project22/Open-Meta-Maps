About Open Meta Maps

Expert-informed metabolic map generation for genome-scale metabolic models.

Open Meta Maps combines reusable layouts from expert-drawn maps with computational layout algorithms to generate metabolic network maps automatically, for exploration, visualisation and sharing, while keeping the reaction information of your model.

How It Works

1

Learn from expert maps

Reusable pathway structures are extracted from expert-drawn metabolic maps; their reaction identity, network connections and geometry are stored in an internal knowledge base.

2

Transfer expert layouts

For an uploaded model, matching structures are retrieved and their geometry is reused where it fits. Exact reaction matches, topology-only references and EC suggestions are kept clearly apart.

3

Complete and optimise

Reactions without reusable expert geometry are placed by the algorithm; pathway organisation, Bézier connections, label placement and layout-quality assessment follow.

Every generated map passes an integrity check that verifies that reactions, stoichiometry and compartment information are preserved. This is an integrity check, not a validation of the biological correctness of the model.

Module instances by source

escher 9,098 · reactome 3,405 · wikipathways 4,718

Layout variants (6,094) outnumber independent structures (4,129) because they are counted over a different scope: variants are distinct drawings of the 3,127 structural families counted over all 17,221 module instances, including the overlapping windows, whereas independent structures are only the module instances not contained in a larger module of the same source map; among those there are 1,232 families and 1,712 layout variants.

Reaction groups are a lower bound: reaction identities from different namespaces are not equated, and geometry-only modules have no reaction identity.

Reproducibility & Transparency

Every result contains:

  • Reaction provenance: whether the geometry was reused from an expert map, only a topology / EC reference, or generated by the algorithm
  • Layout quality
  • Generation settings
  • Licensing information

Expert-geometry reuse and topology-only references are reported separately and are never merged into a single reuse rate.
